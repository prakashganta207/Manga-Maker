The alarm screamed across the night market as Kenji vaulted onto the rooftops, a small silver box clutched to his chest. Drones swept the alley below, their searchlights slicing through the steam.
"Give it back, Kenji!" Aya shouted, landing on the tiles behind him. She was the fastest courier in the district, and she was gaining.
Kenji leapt the gap between two towers and crashed onto a water tank. Aya followed without slowing down. Her boot struck the metal with a loud clang.
"You don't know what's inside!" he yelled.
At the edge of the last roof, Kenji turned and hurled the box toward the river. Aya dove for it and missed by a finger.
The box exploded in a column of white light! For a moment the whole city went silent.
Aya stopped, stunned, her hair whipping in the wind. "What did you do?" she whispered.
Kenji smiled sadly at the glowing water. "I set it free," he said.
