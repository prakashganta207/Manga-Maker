Riku had exactly one job at the Neko Neko Cat Cafe: make sure nobody fed the cats any cake. On his first day, a fat orange cat named Mochi stared at him like a tiny emperor.
"Don't even think about it," Riku warned. Mochi yawned.
At noon, a strict food critic named Ms. Tanabe arrived and ordered the famous strawberry shortcake. The whole cafe held its breath.
Riku carried the cake across the room with trembling hands. Mochi crouched on the bookshelf, tail twitching.
"No. No, no, no," Riku whispered.
Mochi launched himself through the air like a furry cannonball! Riku spun, slipped on a toy mouse, and crashed onto the floor.
The cake flew upward, turned once, and landed perfectly on Ms. Tanabe's plate. Mochi landed perfectly on Ms. Tanabe's lap.
The critic looked at the cake, then at the cat, then at Riku on the floor. "Five stars," she said. "For the show."
Mochi purred. Riku decided he would never understand cats.
